## Episode Summary

In this episode of Higher Ed Hot Takes, Joel Goodman tackles the mounting pressures facing universities worldwide: declining enrollment, rising inflation, government hostility, and the resulting wave of job losses. Drawing from personal experience and industry data, Joel reveals how seemingly small decisions-especially around website performance-can have massive financial consequences for institutions.

### Key Topics Covered

- The global higher ed crisis: job cuts, closures, and financial strain
- Personal reflection on surviving staff redundancies in higher ed
- How ego and poor spending decisions (like unnecessary tech upgrades and cosmetic projects) undermine student and staff needs
- The overlooked importance of website performance in university marketing and recruitment
- Bravery Media’s 2025 SpeedyU benchmarking data: alarming stats on slow higher ed websites across the US, UK, Canada, Ireland, Australia, and New Zealand
- The real-world cost: Google research shows 53% of mobile users leave if a site takes over 3 seconds to load-costing institutions millions in lost tuition and donations
- Common website performance killers:
  - Heavy homepage videos and improper video hosting
  - Excessive tracking pixels (e.g., Facebook) that slow down sites and hurt conversions
  - Outdated codebases (Bootstrap, jQuery, old CMS platforms)
- Why performance is non-negotiable for web marketing and enrollment success
- Actionable advice for universities:
  - Remove or properly optimize large videos
  - Minimize third-party trackers and pixels
  - Modernize code and CMS platforms
  - Leverage in-house developers for low-cost, high-impact fixes
- Bravery Media’s ongoing speed and accessibility testing (SpeedyU.com) and offers for deeper audits or custom checklists
